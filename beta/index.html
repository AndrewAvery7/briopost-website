Get started

Three steps to your first post.

  1. 1

    Sign in

    Open my.briopost.com and sign in with the email address you signed up with. Use Google, LinkedIn, or ask for a sign-in link by email.

  2. 2

    Add your brand

    Open Brand kits and import your colors, fonts and logo from your website, or set them by hand. Every new post starts from them.

  3. 3

    Write one thought

    On the home screen, type an idea, paste your notes or a link, and pick a format. Edit until it sounds like you.

Good to know

Short answers for testers.

What happens when my credits run out?

AI actions that use credits pause until they reset next month. You can still view, edit and export everything you’ve made.

Will anything post to LinkedIn by itself?

No. Every post waits for your OK. Nothing goes to LinkedIn until you confirm that post yourself.

Is my work safe?

This is a beta: things can change or break, and data could be lost. Export anything you want to keep. We’ll email you at least 7 days before the beta ends, or before any big change, so you have time to export.

Can I share my account?

Please don’t. The beta is invite-only. If someone you know would like to try it, tell us and we’ll send them an invitation.

Will it cost anything later?

Not without asking you first. When the beta ends we’ll share the prices, and nothing is charged unless you choose a plan.
