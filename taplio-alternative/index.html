A Taplio alternative: how Briopost compares

Taplio is a LinkedIn growth tool: writing, scheduling, engagement, analytics and lead outreach in one place. Briopost does a narrower job: it helps you make carousels, graphics, text posts and polls in your own brand, and posts them only after your OK. Which one fits depends on how much of that list you use.

Our verdict

Our verdict: if you mainly make posts, choose Briopost

Taplio is strong when you use all of it. Most people who look for an alternative mainly want good posts, and for that job we think Briopost is the better choice. We make Briopost, so here are the reasons, each one checkable in the table below.

  • Free while Taplio costs $39 to $199 a month. The Briopost beta is free, with 300 AI credits a month and no card.
  • AI writing included from day one. Taplio’s Starter plan lists limited AI, with AI writing listed from the $69 Growth plan. In Briopost, AI drafting is in every account, with the cost shown before each action.
  • Deeper design. 150 carousel designs, 100 graphic designs in five sizes and a real slide editor with charts, icons and big numbers, all in your brand kit.
  • Nothing done in your name without you. Every post waits for your confirm click, and Briopost never comments, messages or sends connection requests for you.

Stay with Taplio if you rely on its engagement feeds, lead outreach or live analytics every week. Briopost doesn’t offer those.

What Taplio is

Taplio describes itself as an all-in-one, AI-powered tool to grow your brand on LinkedIn: create posts, schedule them, engage with your network and track results. Its footer says it is made by lempire.

On its pricing page, every plan includes post scheduling on a calendar, analytics, a searchable library of 5M+ posts, polls, an automatic first comment, a Chrome extension, an API and a connection for AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT. Higher plans add AI writing, smart replies, team features, more comments, and lead outreach: a lead finder, automated DMs and automated connection requests.

Taplio also makes carousels. Its carousel page says it can turn an idea, a post, an article or a YouTube video into slides, apply your colors and logo, and export a PDF or schedule it.

Why people look at other tools

Taplio is a full kit, and that is the point of it. People who compare alternatives usually have one of three reasons:

  • Price. On the day we checked, Taplio’s plans were $39, $69 and $199 a month, billed monthly, with 25% off yearly. The Starter plan lists limited AI; writing posts with AI is listed from the Growth plan.
  • Scope. If you mainly need good carousels and posts, you may not use the engagement feeds, lead finder or outreach tools that the higher plans are built around.
  • Focus. Some people want a tool that helps them make posts and leaves commenting, messaging and connection requests to them.

If you use most of what Taplio offers, those reasons may not apply to you. See when to stay with Taplio.

How Briopost is different

  • Design depth. 150 carousel designs and 100 graphic designs in your brand kit, and a real slide editor with charts, about 200 icons, comparisons, cards and big numbers.
  • Start from what you have. A few words, notes, a link, a PDF or up to three minutes of talking becomes a first draft you edit. The recording isn’t kept.
  • Cost first. You see what each AI action will cost before you run it, and you choose Standard or Best each time.
  • Your OK on every post. Nothing posts without your confirm click. Change a post after confirming it, and you confirm again.
  • No activity on your behalf. Briopost doesn’t like, comment on or message other people, and doesn’t send connection requests.
  • Client review links. Clients approve or comment without an account.

What Briopost doesn’t have: engagement feeds, smart replies, lead tools, a library of other people’s posts, or a browser extension (it has a “Send to Briopost” bookmark instead). Live LinkedIn statistics and Company Page posting wait on LinkedIn’s approval; today you can upload LinkedIn’s own analytics file.

Taplio or Briopost?

Stay with Taplio if…

  • You use its engagement feeds, smart replies or lead tools every week.
  • You want a library of other people’s posts to study.
  • You want live analytics inside the tool today.
  • You like working from a Chrome extension inside LinkedIn.
  • Your team or agency already runs on it.

Choose Briopost if…

  • You mainly need carousels, graphics and posts that look like your brand.
  • You want to confirm every post yourself, with nothing posted or sent on your behalf.
  • You want to see what each AI action costs before it runs.
  • Clients need to approve posts without making an account.
  • You want to try it free during the beta.

You don’t have to choose only one. Some people make carousels in one tool and schedule in another.

Questions

Taplio alternative questions

What is a good Taplio alternative?

It depends on what you use Taplio for. If you mainly need carousels, graphics and posts in your own brand, a studio like Briopost covers that. If you rely on engagement, analytics and lead outreach, look for another all-in-one growth tool instead.

How much does Taplio cost?

On November 25, 2026, Taplio listed Starter at $39 a month, Growth at $69 a month and Pro at $199 a month, billed monthly, with 25% off yearly and a 7-day free trial.

Is Briopost free?

Yes, during the beta, by invitation, with 300 AI credits a month. Nothing is charged, and pricing will be shared before anyone pays.

Does Briopost send comments, DMs or connection requests?

No. Briopost doesn’t like, comment on or message other people, and doesn’t send connection requests. The only comments it sends are ones you wrote for your own post, and they are part of what you confirm.

Can Briopost post to LinkedIn for me?

Yes, to your own LinkedIn profile, at the time you choose, after you confirm the post. Company Page posting waits on LinkedIn’s approval.
