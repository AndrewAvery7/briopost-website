Five post ideas

Five posts you could write this month.

Each one works on its own, with or without Briopost. Use your own stories and numbers; the opening lines are only examples.

  1. The question you ask every new client

    Pick the one question you always ask in a first meeting. Say why you ask it and what the answers usually tell you.

    A first line to start fromEvery first meeting, I ask the same question: what have you already tried?

    Format: Carousel, one slide for the question, one for each common answer, one for what you do next.

  2. A mistake you see every week

    Describe a common mistake in your field without naming the client. Say what it costs and give the smallest fix you know.

    A first line to start fromMost teams I meet set goals in January and never look at them again.

    Format: Text post, short, or a do-and-don’t comparison slide.

  3. Your method, one step per slide

    If you work through a set of steps, give each step its own slide with one sentence and one example from real work.

    A first line to start fromThe four questions I ask before any price change.

    Format: Carousel, numbered slides, one step each.

  4. Before and after, with permission

    Show where a client started and where they ended up. Use numbers only if the client agreed, and leave names out unless you have their written OK.

    A first line to start fromSix months ago this team spent every Friday fixing reports.

    Format: Comparison or chart, built from the client’s real numbers.

  5. Ask your audience

    Run a poll on a choice your clients face. A week later, post what the votes and the comments taught you.

    A first line to start fromQuick poll for founders: who writes your sales emails?

    Format: Poll, up to four options, then a text post with what you learned.

Questions

Short answers.

What should a consultant or coach post on LinkedIn?

Posts that show how you think: the questions you ask, the mistakes you see, the steps of your method, and what changed for a client who agreed to be mentioned. One useful idea per post is enough.

Will Briopost post for me?

No. Every post waits for your OK, and nothing goes to LinkedIn until you confirm it yourself. See what we never do.

How do I test the first line of a post?

Write up to three versions in the free hook preview on this page. It shows roughly where “…see more” cuts each one on a phone, so you can keep the one that makes its point first.
