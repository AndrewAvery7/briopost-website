Five post ideas

Five posts you could write this month.

Each one works on its own, with or without Briopost. Use your own stories and numbers; the opening lines are only examples.

  1. What you changed this month, and why

    Pick one change to your product or service and the customer problem behind it. Say what you will watch to know it worked.

    A first line to start fromWe changed one thing this month because three customers asked the same question.

    Format: Carousel, the problem, the change, what comes next.

  2. The question customers ask most

    Answer it in public. Everyone who wondered and never asked gets the answer too.

    A first line to start fromThe question we hear on almost every sales call: how long does setup take?

    Format: Text post, a plain answer in a few short paragraphs.

  3. A decision and the reason behind it

    A price change, a first hire, a feature you said no to. People trust a business more when they can follow its thinking.

    A first line to start fromWe said no to our biggest customer’s feature request. Here is why.

    Format: Text post, or a two-sided comparison slide.

  4. A number you watch every week

    Share a number you are comfortable making public and explain why it matters more than the obvious ones.

    A first line to start fromThe one number I check every Monday morning.

    Format: Chart slide, built from your own numbers.

  5. What you would tell yourself a year ago

    Three to five lessons from the last year, each with the moment you learned it.

    A first line to start fromA year ago I thought hiring fast was the answer.

    Format: Carousel, one lesson per slide.

  1. 1

    Write your opening

    Type the first line or two of your post in Hook 1. Two sample hooks are filled in so you can see how it works: replace them with yours.

  2. 2

    Add other versions

    Choose “Add another hook” to try a second or third way to say it. You can compare up to three side by side.

  3. 3

    Check the cut

    Each preview shows roughly where “…see more” falls on a phone, and the line under each box says whether the whole hook shows before it.

  4. 4

    Copy the one that works

    Choose “Copy hook” under the version that makes its point before the cut, and paste it at the top of your post.

Questions

Short answers.

What should a founder post on LinkedIn?

What you are building and why, the questions customers ask, decisions you made and what you learned from them. Write for the customer you want, not for other founders.

Can my team use Briopost too?

Yes. Invite your team to your workspace. Owners, admins and members each get the right controls.

How do I test the first line of a post?

Write up to three versions in the free hook preview on this page. It shows roughly where “…see more” cuts each one on a phone, so you can keep the one that makes its point first.
