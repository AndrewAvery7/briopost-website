Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Download as PDF

Briopost is preparing to open to customers. This document describes how Briopost works for customers from the day accounts open, and how this website works today.

1. Who we are

Briopost is a studio for planning, writing and designing LinkedIn posts: carousels, graphics, text posts and polls. Briopost is operated by Andrew Avery. You can reach us at info@briopost.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and sign-in details.
  • What you create and give us: post drafts, carousels, graphics, brand kits (logos, colours, fonts, writing style), pictures you upload, and notes, links or documents you ask Briopost to turn into posts.
  • Your LinkedIn connection: if you connect LinkedIn, we store the authorisation LinkedIn gives us so we can publish, and your LinkedIn name and profile picture so your posts show as yours. We use it only to publish a post that you have confirmed.
  • Usage records: which actions you ran and what each AI action cost, so we can show you your spend.
  • Messages you send us: if you write to us, we keep the message and your address so we can reply.
  • Technical logs: basic request details (such as IP address and browser type) kept briefly to run and protect the service.

3. How we use it

To provide Briopost: to create and store your content, generate drafts and pictures when you ask, schedule posts, publish the posts you confirm, show your usage and cost, send the service emails described below, answer your questions, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. AI processing

When you ask Briopost to write or design something, the text, links or pictures needed for that request are sent to our AI providers (listed below) and their results come back to you. We send only what the request needs, and we show the approximate cost before a paid AI action runs. The providers' own terms govern how they handle the data they process for us.

5. Publishing to LinkedIn

Briopost publishes to LinkedIn only through LinkedIn's official interface, and only after you confirm each individual post yourself. You can disconnect LinkedIn at any time, and you can also remove Briopost's access from your LinkedIn settings. Briopost is independent of LinkedIn and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.

6. Email we send you

We send service email only, from planner@briopost.com or info@briopost.com: account messages, reminders you switch on (for example, a reminder to plan this week's post) and a weekly summary of post ideas if you turn it on. We send no marketing without a separate opt-in. Every reminder tells you how to change or turn it off, and you can also reply to ask us to stop. See our Email Policy.

8. This website

briopost.com has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. The interactive examples run in your browser and send nothing. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log visitors' IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect it, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

9. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your content and account while your account is open. You can delete any item yourself. To close your account and have your information deleted, write to info@briopost.com from the address on the account; we will delete it within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law. Backups are overwritten on their normal schedule.

10. Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it or delete it, and turn off any reminder or email at any time. Write to info@briopost.com.

11. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections, access to accounts requires sign-in, and LinkedIn authorisations are stored only so we can publish for you. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

12. Children

Briopost is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Changes

When this policy changes we will post the new version here with a new date, and tell account holders by email if the change matters to them.
