Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Download as PDF

Briopost is preparing to open to customers. This document describes how Briopost works for customers from the day accounts open, and how this website works today.

1. Who we are

Briopost is a studio for planning, writing and designing LinkedIn posts: carousels, graphics, text posts and polls. Briopost is operated by Briopost LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, 32 North Gould Street, Sheridan, WY 82801. You can reach us at info@briopost.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and sign-in details.
  • What you create and give us: post drafts, carousels, graphics, brand kits (logos, colors, fonts, writing style), pictures you upload, and notes, links or documents you ask Briopost to turn into posts.
  • Your LinkedIn connection: if you connect LinkedIn, we store the authorization LinkedIn gives us so we can publish, and your LinkedIn name and profile picture so your posts show as yours. We use it only to publish a post that you have confirmed.
  • Billing: if you subscribe, our payment provider, Stripe, collects your card details and we never see or store the full card number. We keep your plan, trial and subscription status, the dates and amounts of charges and refunds, the AI credit top-ups you buy, and a Stripe customer reference.
  • Workspaces: if you invite teammates to a workspace, we keep their names and email addresses, and their role (owner, admin or member).
  • Usage records: which actions you ran, what each AI action cost, and how many AI credits you have left, so we can show you your credits and spend.
  • Waitlist: if you join the waitlist on briopost.com, your first name and email address, what best describes you if you choose to say (for example consultant, founder, marketer or job seeker), which link brought you to the form if it had one, the dates you joined and confirmed, and whether you are confirmed or have unsubscribed. We do not store your IP address with your entry. To stop abuse of the form we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of it for less than a day.
  • Messages you send us: if you write to us, we keep the message and your address so we can reply.
  • Technical logs: basic request details (such as IP address and browser type) kept briefly to run and protect the service.

3. How we use it

To provide Briopost: to create and store your content, generate drafts and pictures when you ask, schedule posts, publish the posts you confirm, show your usage, credits and cost, send the service emails described below, answer your questions, and keep the service secure. We use waitlist details only to tell you when Briopost opens and to ask whether you want early access; we send no other marketing without a separate opt-in. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. AI processing

Briopost offers AI in two ways. On the plans with AI included, Briopost pays for the AI, and each action uses some of your monthly AI credits. When you ask Briopost to write or design something, the text, links or pictures needed for that request are sent to Briopost's own AI providers, under Briopost's own accounts with them: Anthropic for writing and fal.ai for pictures. On the own-key plan, those requests go instead to the provider whose key you added (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or fal.ai), under your key, and you pay that provider directly.

Either way, the result comes back to you, we send only what the request needs, and we show the cost (in credits, or in dollars on your own key) before a paid AI action runs. The provider's own terms govern how it handles the data. We keep a record of each AI action and what it cost, so we can show you your credits and spend and keep the service within its limits.

5. Publishing to LinkedIn

Briopost publishes to LinkedIn only through LinkedIn's official interface, and only after you confirm each individual post yourself. You can disconnect LinkedIn at any time, and you can also remove Briopost's access from your LinkedIn settings. Briopost is independent of LinkedIn and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.

6. Email we send you

We send service email only, from planner@briopost.com, hello@briopost.com or info@briopost.com: account messages, reminders you switch on (for example, a reminder to plan this week's post), a weekly summary of post ideas if you turn it on, and, if you join the waitlist, the message asking you to confirm your email address and a message when Briopost opens. We send no marketing without a separate opt-in. Every reminder tells you how to change or turn it off, and you can also reply to ask us to stop. See our Email Policy.

8. This website

briopost.com has no accounts and no advertising. It uses Google Analytics, a service of Google LLC, for every visitor. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to measure visits: which pages you open, how you arrived (for example from LinkedIn, a search or a link with a campaign tag), what you do on the site (such as scrolling, pressing a button, starting or sending the waitlist form, or downloading a file), the type of device and browser, and your approximate location. This information is shared with Google, which processes it under the Google Privacy Policy; you can see how Google uses it at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can block or delete the cookies in your browser settings, or stop Google Analytics on every website with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. We use what Google Analytics shows us only as statistics, to understand how the site is used and improve it.

The site also counts its own visits: for each page view we record the page, the website that linked to it (if any), the country the visit came from and the date, kept only as totals. We store no IP address and nothing that identifies you. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and measures how fast pages load without using cookies or storing anything on your device; it records the page, the referring website, the browser and device type, and the country the visit came from. Google Search Console tells us how the site appears in Google search (searches, clicks and positions); it gives us totals only, never who searched. We also run Google's PageSpeed Insights test on the site's pages, which sends no information about you.

It has one form, the waitlist form, which asks for your first name, your email address and, if you choose, what best describes you. When you send it, those details go to Briopost's own database (hosted by Cloudflare); sections 2 and 3 explain what we do with them. Google Analytics records that the form was started or sent, but never what you typed. The interactive examples run in your browser and send nothing. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log visitors' IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect it, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

9. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your content and account while your account is open. You can delete any item yourself. You can delete your whole account yourself (Account page, Delete my account), or write to info@briopost.com from the address on the account and we will do it for you.

30 days to change your mind. A deleted account is first put on hold for 30 days, and we email you the date it will be deleted. While it is on hold, no one can open it and nothing is published or run for it, but nothing is deleted: if you sign in within those 30 days and choose Reactivate, everything comes back as you left it, including your content, pictures, brand kits and saved AI keys. Posts whose time passed while it was on hold are not published late; they wait for you to choose a new time. After the 30 days, we delete the account, its content and its stored files for good, except for records we must keep by law, such as payment and tax records. A team workspace its owner deletes is held the same way, and the owner can restore it within the 30 days. Backups are overwritten on their normal schedule: we keep a backup copy of stored files (such as pictures) so they can be put back after a mistake, and a file's backup copy is removed 30 days after the file itself is deleted.

Waitlist entries: if you never confirm your email address, we delete the entry after 30 days. If you confirm, we keep it until you unsubscribe, ask us to delete it, or Briopost opens and you decide whether to create an account. If you unsubscribe, we keep your email address marked as unsubscribed so that we do not email you again; ask us and we will delete it completely. To have your waitlist entry deleted, write to info@briopost.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete it within 30 days.

10. Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it or delete it, and turn off any reminder or email, or leave the waitlist, at any time. Write to info@briopost.com.

11. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections, access to accounts requires sign-in, and LinkedIn authorizations are stored only so we can publish for you. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

12. Children

Briopost is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Changes

When this policy changes we will post the new version here with a new date, and tell account holders by email if the change matters to them.
