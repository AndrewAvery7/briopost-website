Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Download as PDF

Briopost is preparing to open to customers. This document describes how Briopost works for customers from the day accounts open, and how this website works today.

1. Who we are

Briopost is a studio for planning, writing and designing LinkedIn posts: carousels, graphics, text posts and polls. Briopost is operated by Briopost LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, 32 North Gould Street, Sheridan, WY 82801. You can reach us at info@briopost.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and sign-in details.
  • What you create and give us: post drafts, carousels, graphics, brand kits (logos, colors, fonts, writing style, and any past posts you paste so Briopost can learn your voice, with the voice profile written from them), pictures you upload, and notes, links or documents you ask Briopost to turn into posts.
  • Your LinkedIn connection: if you connect LinkedIn, we store the authorization LinkedIn gives us, and your LinkedIn name and profile picture so your posts show as yours. We use it only for what you ask Briopost to do on LinkedIn: to publish a post you have confirmed, with a first comment or reshare if you set one up and approve it, and, if you connect a LinkedIn Page you administer, to publish there. We never publish anything you have not confirmed.
  • Your LinkedIn statistics: if you switch on LinkedIn statistics, you sign in to LinkedIn a second time, separately from publishing, and Briopost reads the performance numbers of your own LinkedIn posts and profile (such as impressions, members reached, reactions, comments and reshares) through LinkedIn's official interface, about once a day and when you press Refresh. You can also upload the analytics file you download from LinkedIn: your browser reads it and sends us only the numbers in it, with the file's name and the links and dates of your posts, never the file itself. We use these numbers only to show you your own analytics, keep them while your account is open, and delete them with your account. You can disconnect LinkedIn statistics, or remove uploaded numbers, at any time.
  • Billing: if you subscribe or buy a top-up, our payment provider, Stripe, collects your card details and billing details (such as your billing address, and a tax ID if you give one), and we never see or store the full card number. We keep your plan, trial and subscription status, the dates and amounts of charges and refunds, the AI credit top-ups you buy, the dates of any break you take, the reason you give for canceling or taking a break if you choose to give one, and a Stripe customer reference.
  • Workspaces: if you invite teammates to a workspace, we keep their names and email addresses, and their role (owner, admin or member).
  • Usage records: which actions you ran, what each AI action cost, and how many AI credits you have left, so we can show you your credits and spend. Also your weekly posting goal and streak, your weekly plan settings, and any thumbs up or down you give an AI draft (with your short comment, if you write one; the draft itself is not kept).
  • Waitlist: if you join the waitlist on briopost.com, your first name and email address, what best describes you if you choose to say (for example consultant, founder, marketer or job seeker), which link brought you to the form if it had one, the dates you joined and confirmed, and whether you are confirmed or have unsubscribed. We do not store your IP address with your entry. To stop abuse of the form we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of it for less than a day.
  • Messages you send us: if you write to us, we keep the message and your address so we can reply.
  • Technical logs: basic request details (such as IP address and browser type) that our hosting provider, Cloudflare, records to run and protect the service. They are deleted automatically after no more than 30 days. Separately, we keep a record of errors in the app, with personal details such as email addresses, cookies and keys removed, for up to 90 days so we can fix problems.

Cookies and storage in the app. The Briopost app uses only the cookies it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in (named bp_customer, for up to 30 days, or until you sign out), and short-lived ones, lasting an hour or less, that protect a sign-in, an invitation or a LinkedIn connection while it is in progress. It also keeps your preferences (such as light or dark mode) and text you have not saved yet in your browser's own storage, under names that start with pl-, so they are still there when you come back; they stay on your device until you choose to save or send them. The app has no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no tracking by other companies. For cookies on our public website, see section 8.

3. How we use it

To provide Briopost: to create and store your content, generate drafts and pictures when you ask, schedule posts, publish the posts you confirm, show your LinkedIn statistics if you switch them on, show your usage, credits and cost, send the service emails described below, answer your questions, and keep the service secure. We use waitlist details only to tell you when Briopost opens and to ask whether you want early access. We send no marketing without a separate opt-in, with one exception: if a paid plan of yours ends, we may send up to two short reminders (about 30 and 60 days later) that your work is still in your account and how to restart, export or delete it, which mention the current plans and prices; each has a one-click unsubscribe link. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. AI processing

Briopost offers AI in two ways. On the plans with AI included, Briopost pays for the AI, and each action uses some of your monthly AI credits. When you ask Briopost to write or design something, the text, links or pictures needed for that request are sent to Briopost's own AI providers, under Briopost's own accounts with them: Anthropic for writing and fal.ai for pictures. On the own-key plan, those requests go instead to the provider whose key you added (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or fal.ai), under your key, and you pay that provider directly.

Voice notes. If you use Talk it through to record a voice note for a post, the recording is sent to fal.ai to be turned into text, under Briopost's own account when you use AI credits or under your own fal.ai key on the own-key plan. We delete the recording from our storage as soon as it has been turned into text; we never keep the audio. The text comes back to you to edit, and we keep it only if you put it in a post or draft.

Either way, the result comes back to you, we send only what the request needs, and we show the cost (in credits, or in dollars on your own key) before a paid AI action runs. The provider's own terms govern how it handles the data. We keep a record of each AI action and what it cost, so we can show you your credits and spend and keep the service within its limits.

5. Publishing to LinkedIn

Briopost publishes to LinkedIn only through LinkedIn's official interface, and only after you confirm each individual post yourself. You can disconnect LinkedIn at any time, and you can also remove Briopost's access from your LinkedIn settings. Briopost is independent of LinkedIn and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.

6. Email we send you

We send service email from planner@briopost.com, hello@briopost.com or info@briopost.com: account messages, reminders you switch on (for example, a reminder to plan this week's post), a weekly summary of post ideas if you turn it on, a Friday progress email once you set a weekly posting goal (on unless you turn it off in Settings), an email when your weekly plan is ready if you switched that on, and, if you join the waitlist, the message asking you to confirm your email address and a message when Briopost opens. We send no marketing without a separate opt-in; the only exception is the two reminders after a paid plan ends, described in section 3. Every reminder tells you how to change or turn it off, and you can also reply to ask us to stop. See our Email Policy.

8. This website

briopost.com has no accounts and no advertising. It uses Google Analytics, a service of Google LLC, for every visitor. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to measure visits: which pages you open, how you arrived (for example from LinkedIn, a search or a link with a campaign tag), what you do on the site (such as scrolling, pressing a button, starting or sending the waitlist form, or downloading a file), the type of device and browser, and your approximate location. This information is shared with Google, which processes it under the Google Privacy Policy; you can see how Google uses it at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can block or delete the cookies in your browser settings, or stop Google Analytics on every website with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. We use what Google Analytics shows us only as statistics, to understand how the site is used and improve it.

The site also counts its own visits: for each page view we record the page, the website that linked to it (if any), the country the visit came from and the date, kept only as totals. We store no IP address and nothing that identifies you. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and measures how fast pages load without using cookies or storing anything on your device; it records the page, the referring website, the browser and device type, and the country the visit came from. Google Search Console tells us how the site appears in Google search (searches, clicks and positions); it gives us totals only, never who searched. We also run Google's PageSpeed Insights test on the site's pages, which sends no information about you.

It has one form, the waitlist form, which asks for your first name, your email address and, if you choose, what best describes you. When you send it, those details go to Briopost's own database (hosted by Cloudflare); sections 2 and 3 explain what we do with them. Google Analytics records that the form was started or sent, but never what you typed. The interactive examples run in your browser and send nothing. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log visitors' IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect it, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

9. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your content and account while your account is open. You can delete any item yourself. You can delete your whole account yourself (Account page, Delete my account), or write to info@briopost.com from the address on the account and we will do it for you.

30 days to change your mind. A deleted account is first put on hold for 30 days, and we email you the date it will be deleted. While it is on hold, no one can open it and nothing is published or run for it, but nothing is deleted: if you sign in within those 30 days and choose Reactivate, everything comes back as you left it, including your content, pictures, brand kits and saved AI keys. Posts whose time passed while it was on hold are not published late; they wait for you to choose a new time. After the 30 days, we delete the account, its content and its stored files for good, except for records we must keep by law, such as payment and tax records. A team workspace its owner deletes is held the same way, and the owner can restore it within the 30 days. Backups are overwritten on their normal schedule: we keep a backup copy of stored files (such as pictures) so they can be put back after a mistake, and a file's backup copy is removed 30 days after the file itself is deleted.

Waitlist entries: if you never confirm your email address, we delete the entry after 30 days. If you confirm, we keep it until you unsubscribe, ask us to delete it, or Briopost opens and you decide whether to create an account. If you unsubscribe, we keep your email address marked as unsubscribed so that we do not email you again; ask us and we will delete it completely. To have your waitlist entry deleted, write to info@briopost.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete it within 30 days.

10. Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it or delete it, and turn off any reminder or email, or leave the waitlist, at any time. Write to info@briopost.com. We answer every request within 30 days, and may ask you to write from the email address on your account (or the one you joined the waitlist with) so we know the request is yours. Many of these you can also do yourself: Settings has Download my data, and the Account page has Delete my account.

Where your information is kept. Briopost is run from the United States. Your account and content are stored with Cloudflare, and the providers in section 7 handle what they need in the United States or in other countries where they work. If you use Briopost from outside the United States, your information is transferred to and handled in the United States.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. We use your information to provide the service you signed up for (our contract with you); to keep it secure, prevent abuse, send account messages and improve it (our legitimate interests); because the law requires it, for example payment and tax records; and, for the waitlist and optional emails, because you agreed, which you can withdraw at any time. You can ask for access, a copy, correction or deletion, object to or ask us to limit how we use your information, and complain to your local data protection authority.

If you live in California. You can ask to know what personal information we hold about you and where it came from, ask for a copy, and ask us to correct or delete it, and we will not treat you differently for asking. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. The categories we collect are those in section 2, for the purposes in section 3, shared only with the providers in section 7.

11. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections, access to accounts requires sign-in, and LinkedIn authorizations are stored encrypted and used only for the LinkedIn actions you ask for (section 2). No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

12. Children

Briopost is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Changes

When this policy changes we will post the new version here with a new date, and tell account holders by email if the change matters to them.
