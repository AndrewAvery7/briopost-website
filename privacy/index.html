Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Who we are

Briopost is a studio for planning and writing LinkedIn posts: carousels, graphics, text posts and polls. This website, briopost.com, is public. The Briopost app at app.briopost.com is currently a private, password-protected tool that is not open to the public. You can reach us at info@briopost.com.

This website

This website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. The interactive examples on the home page are scripted demonstrations that run in your browser; they do not send anything anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the site, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us

Mail sent to info@briopost.com is received through Cloudflare Email Routing and forwarded to the inbox of Briopost's owner. We use your address and message only to reply to you. We do not sell or share them, and we will delete them on request.

Email that Briopost sends

Briopost sends a small number of service emails from planner@briopost.com: a reminder to plan a LinkedIn post, and a weekly summary of post ideas. They go only to the person who switched those reminders on. Today that is the owner of Briopost, at his own address. We send no marketing, no newsletters and nothing to purchased or shared lists. Every reminder says how to change or pause reminders.

We use Mailgun (Mailgun Technologies, Inc.) to deliver these emails. Mailgun processes the recipient's address and the message in order to deliver it.

LinkedIn

Briopost is independent of LinkedIn and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn. Briopost never publishes a post to LinkedIn unless the owner has confirmed that specific post himself.

When the app opens to the public

Before anyone other than the owner can sign up, this policy will be updated to list exactly what the app collects, why, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to info@briopost.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
