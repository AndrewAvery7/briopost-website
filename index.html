How it works

From a rough idea to a finished post. You decide every step.

  1. 1

    Start with an idea

    Paste your notes, a link or a rough thought.

  2. 2

  4. 4

    You read the final post and give your OK.

  5. 5

    It posts

    Only after your OK, at the time you choose.

Questions

Good questions. Short answers.

Do I need my own AI account?

Yes. Briopost connects to your own AI and image accounts, so you pay the provider directly. Before each AI action, Briopost shows you the approximate cost.

Does anything post without me?

Never. Every post waits for your OK. Nothing goes to LinkedIn until you confirm it yourself.

When does it open?

Invites go out first, then Briopost opens to everyone. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when it opens.

Is it made by LinkedIn?

No. Briopost is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.

What will it cost?

We’ll share pricing before anyone pays. The beta is free.
