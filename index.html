How it works

Three steps, from a rough idea to a post you’ll be happy to share.

  1. 1

    Start from anything

    A few words, a link, a PDF, your notes or a voice note. Talk for up to three minutes; the recording isn’t kept.

  2. 2

    Make it yours

    Save your colors, fonts and logo once in a brand kit. Start from 150 carousel designs and every slide follows your kit.

  3. 3

    Plan it and give your OK

    Put it on your calendar. When the time comes, it waits for your click, and you can take your OK back.

Questions

Will Briopost post for me?

No. Nothing posts without your OK. Every post waits on your calendar until you confirm it.

Do I have to connect my LinkedIn account?

No. Connecting LinkedIn is optional. You can download a carousel as a PDF or as images and post it yourself. Connect LinkedIn only if you want Briopost to post at the time you picked, after your OK.

Is it free?

The beta is free, with 300 AI credits a month. Nothing is charged. We’ll share pricing before anyone pays.

How do I become a beta tester?

Leave your first name and email in the form below. We confirm your email, then send beta invitations in small groups.

Is Briopost part of LinkedIn?

No. Briopost is independent of LinkedIn and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by it.

Do I need my own AI account?

No. During the beta, AI credits are included, and you see the cost in credits before each AI action.

Can I bring my own AI key?

Yes: your own Claude or OpenAI key, or any service that works like OpenAI’s, such as DeepSeek or Kimi. It’s stored encrypted and never shown again.

What can I make?

Carousels, single graphics, text posts and polls, from 150 carousel designs and 100 graphic designs, in your own brand kit. Download them as a PDF, as images or as a short video.

What is a LinkedIn carousel?

A post people swipe through, one slide at a time. LinkedIn builds it from a document, usually a PDF. Briopost designs the slides and exports a PDF ready to upload.

What size should a LinkedIn carousel be?

Most people use portrait, 1080 by 1350 pixels, or square, 1080 by 1080. Briopost makes both, plus a tall story size.

Can I post to a Company Page?

Not yet. Company Pages wait on LinkedIn’s approval. Today you can post to your own LinkedIn profile, with your OK.

Is there a phone app?

Not yet. Apps for iPhone and Android are planned once the studio is settled. Beta testers will hear first.

Can I leave with my work?

Yes. Download everything you made, or delete your account, any time.
