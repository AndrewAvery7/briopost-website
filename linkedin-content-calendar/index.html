How to plan a month of LinkedIn posts

  1. Pick a pace you can keep. Twice a week for six months is easier to keep up than a post every day for two weeks.
  2. Choose two or three topics you know well, and rotate between them.
  3. Mix the formats. A carousel for the big idea, a text post for a story, a poll when you want to ask.
  4. Write in batches. An hour on Monday can fill the week.
  5. Leave open slots for news and for things that happen to you.
  6. Reuse what worked. A good post can run again a few weeks later in new words.

What the Briopost calendar does

  • Month, week, day or list

    See every post on a calendar, in the view that suits you.

  • Plan a series

    Plan a run of posts in one go. They land as drafts, and you see the cost of each one first.

  • Repeats in fresh words

    Repeat a good post every 1 to 12 weeks, up to 6 times. Each repeat is reworded and waits for its own OK.

  • First comment and follow-ups

    Add a first comment that goes out with the post, and plan follow-up comments or a reshare for after it’s live.

  • Rhythm and nudges

    Set your posting rhythm and get a nudge when a slot is empty. Set a weekly goal and keep your streak.

  • Needs your OK

    Everything waiting for your OK sits in one list, so nothing slips through.

Every post needs your OK

Connect your LinkedIn profile and give each post a day and time. A post goes out at that time only if you have confirmed it. Until then it is marked “Needs your OK” and it waits.

Planning for a client? Send them a review link first. They approve or comment without an account, and you still give the final OK.

Questions

Calendar questions

How often should I post on LinkedIn?

It varies. Pick a pace you can keep for months, such as once or twice a week, and plan to it. A steady rhythm is easier to keep than bursts.

Can you schedule a LinkedIn post?

Yes. In Briopost you put the post on the calendar at the day and time you want. It goes out only after you confirm it, so nothing posts by surprise.

What is the best time to post on LinkedIn?

It depends on when your readers are online, which varies by audience. Pick a time, keep it steady for a few weeks, and compare.

Can I repeat a post that did well?

Yes. Set it to repeat every 1 to 12 weeks, up to 6 times. Each repeat gets fresh wording and needs its own OK.
