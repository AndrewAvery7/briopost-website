A Contentdrips alternative: how Briopost compares

Contentdrips makes carousels, graphics and posts for LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok. If you are weighing it against Briopost, here is what each one does, what each costs, and who each one suits, in plain terms.

By the Briopost team · Last updated November 25, 2026 · Facts about Contentdrips come from its own website, checked November 25, 2026.

What Contentdrips is

Contentdrips is a social media post maker with an AI design agent and a design editor. You give it a topic, a blog post, a YouTube video, a TikTok or your own text, and it writes and designs carousels, graphics and text posts in your brand.

It can publish or schedule to LinkedIn and Instagram, and its Pro plan adds TikTok. It also offers bulk creation from a CSV file, an API and an MCP server for AI assistants. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

What Contentdrips does well

  • Several apps from one screen. Schedule to LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok (all three on Pro and Ultimate).
  • Many ways to start. Topics, blog links, YouTube videos, TikToks, plain text, and a CSV for many posts at once.
  • Writing that learns from you. Its Match My Style feature learns from your past LinkedIn posts.
  • Built for volume. An API (6,000 calls a month on Pro, 25,000 on Ultimate) and an MCP server for assistants like Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor.
  • An easy trial. 7 days free with no credit card, and a 15-day refund policy.

How Briopost is different

Briopost is a studio made only for LinkedIn. It is in a free beta, by invitation. Here is where it takes a different path.

  • Every post waits for your OK. Contentdrips lets you publish immediately or schedule for later. In Briopost, a scheduled post goes out only after you confirm it, and if you change it after confirming, you confirm again.
  • You see the cost before each AI action. Contentdrips uses monthly AI credits with a fixed cost per feature. Briopost shows what each action will cost before it runs, and lets you choose Standard or Best each time.
  • Client review links. Clients can approve or comment on posts through a link, without making an account.
  • LinkedIn only. Briopost does not post to Instagram or TikTok. It focuses on LinkedIn carousels, graphics, text posts and polls, on one calendar with series, repeats, a first comment and follow-up comments.
  • It is still in beta. Briopost is free while the beta runs, by invitation. Contentdrips is an established paid product.

Which one fits you

Stay with Contentdrips if…

  • You post to Instagram and TikTok as well as LinkedIn, from one place.
  • You make posts in bulk from a CSV file or through an API.
  • You want a paid product with fixed monthly credits that is out of beta today.
  • You are happy to publish right away or let scheduled posts go out on their own.

Try Briopost if…

  • You post mainly on LinkedIn and want a studio made only for it.
  • You want every post to wait for your OK before it goes out.
  • You want to see what each AI action costs before you run it.
  • You post for clients and want them to approve through a link, without an account.
  • You are happy to use a product in beta, for free, and tell us what is missing.

Questions

Contentdrips alternative questions

Is Briopost a good Contentdrips alternative?

It depends on what you need. Briopost is made only for LinkedIn and asks for your OK before every post. Contentdrips also schedules to Instagram and TikTok, and offers bulk creation and an API. The table on this page shows the differences.

How much does Contentdrips cost?

Its pricing page lists Starter at $19 a month, Pro at $35 a month and Ultimate at $65 a month, with lower prices when billed yearly and a 7-day free trial. Check its site for current prices.

How much does Briopost cost?

Briopost is free during the beta, by invitation, with 300 AI credits a month. Pricing will be shared before anyone pays.

Does Briopost post to Instagram or TikTok?

No. Briopost is made for LinkedIn. It can export a PDF, images or a short video that you can post anywhere yourself.

Can I bring my own AI key?

In Briopost you can use your own Claude or OpenAI key, or a service that works like OpenAI’s. We did not find an own-key option on Contentdrips’ pricing page.
