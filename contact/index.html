Contact and Support

Last updated November 10, 2026

Download as PDF

Briopost is preparing to open to customers. This document describes how Briopost works for customers from the day accounts open, and how this website works today.

Contact

Email info@briopost.com for questions, support, privacy requests (what we hold, a copy, correction or deletion), and to report a problem with a Briopost email.

Support response times

During the beta we reply within 2 business days, and after the beta within 1 business day. When we are away you will get an automatic reply saying so.

Mailing address

Briopost, 12220 North MacArthur Boulevard, Suite F-19, Oklahoma City, OK 73162

What to include

The email address on your account, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Please never send your password.

Account deletion

To close your account and delete your information, email info@briopost.com from the address on the account. We delete it within 30 days. If you only joined the waitlist, write from the address you signed up with and we will delete your waitlist entry within 30 days. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use briopost.com and Briopost. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. If anything is hard to use with your browser, screen reader or other tools, write to info@briopost.com and tell us what happened, and we will work on a fix.
