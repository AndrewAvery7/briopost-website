Download as PDF

Briopost is preparing to open to customers. This document describes how Briopost works for customers from the day accounts open, and how this website works today.

Contact

Email info@briopost.com for questions, support, privacy requests (what we hold, a copy, correction or deletion), and to report a problem with a Briopost email.

What to include

The email address on your account, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Please never send your password.

Account deletion

To close your account and delete your information, email info@briopost.com from the address on the account. We delete it within 30 days. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
