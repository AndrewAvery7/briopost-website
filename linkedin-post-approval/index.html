Client approval

Let clients approve LinkedIn posts before they go out

Send one link. Your client reads the posts, leaves comments and approves them without signing up for anything. Then you give the final OK.

How a client approval works

  1. Pick the posts

    Choose up to 20 posts for one review link: carousels, text posts, graphics or polls.

  2. Send the link

    Your client opens it in a browser. There’s no account to create. The link stays open for 14 days.

  3. They approve or comment

    Each post can be approved, or get a comment saying what to change.

  4. Edits ask again

    If you change a post after it was approved, the link asks for approval again.

  5. You confirm

    Approval doesn’t post anything. The post goes to LinkedIn only after you confirm it yourself.

Tips for faster client approvals

  • Send posts in batches, a week or two ahead, instead of one at a time.
  • Show the finished post. Clients approve faster when they see the real slides, not a text file.
  • Ask for one decision per post: approve it, or say what to change.
  • Agree on a turnaround, such as two business days, so posts don’t stall.
  • Keep each client separate. One workspace and one brand kit per client keeps colors and voices from mixing.

How to format a post, step by step

  1. 1

    Write or paste your post

    Type in the box above, or paste a draft you already have. Not sure where to start? Choose “Try an example” to fill in a sample post.

  2. 2

    Style a few words

    Select a word or phrase, then choose Bold or Italic (or press Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I). Select some lines and choose Bullets or Numbers to turn them into a list. “Remove styling” puts selected text back to plain.

  3. 3

    Check the preview

    Keep the counter under about 3,000 characters. Switch between Phone and Computer to see roughly where “…see more” cuts the post, and keep your main point above it.

  4. 4

    Copy and paste into LinkedIn

    Choose “Copy post”, start a new post on LinkedIn and paste. The bold and italic words, lists and line breaks come with it.

Questions

Approval questions

Does my client need a Briopost account?

No. Clients open the review link in a browser, then approve posts or leave comments without an account.

How many posts can go in one review link?

Up to 20 posts. A review link stays open for 14 days.

What if I edit a post after the client approved it?

Briopost asks for approval again, so the client signs off on the version that goes out.

Does an approved post go out on its own?

No. Client approval doesn’t post anything. A post goes to LinkedIn only after you confirm it yourself. See what we never do.

Can I manage several clients?

Yes. You can work in more than one workspace, for example one per client, and invite your team with owner, admin or member roles.

Can I format a post before I send it for approval?

Yes. Use the free post formatter on this page to bold a few words, add a list and check the length, then put the post in Briopost and send the review link.
