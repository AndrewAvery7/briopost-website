What makes a post sound like you

Readers can tell when a post was written to fill a slot. Posts that sound like a person usually have a few things in common.

  • A real starting point. Something you saw, did or got asked this week works better than a topic.
  • A first line you would say out loud. Read it to yourself. If you wouldn’t say it, rewrite it.
  • Your own words. Keep the phrases you use with clients, and cut the ones you never use.
  • Short paragraphs. One idea each, with line breaks, so it reads well on a phone.
  • A question you want answered. End with one, and reply to the comments.

A generator can write the draft. The voice has to come from you, which is why Briopost asks for your raw material first.

How to test a hook, step by step

  1. 1

    Write your opening

    Type the first line or two of your post in Hook 1. Two sample hooks are filled in so you can see how it works: replace them with yours.

  2. 2

    Add other versions

    Choose “Add another hook” to try a second or third way to say it. You can compare up to three side by side.

  3. 3

    Check the cut

    Each preview shows roughly where “…see more” falls on a phone, and the line under each box says whether the whole hook shows before it.

  4. 4

    Copy the one that works

    Choose “Copy hook” under the version that makes its point before the cut, and paste it at the top of your post.

Questions

Post generator questions

Will the post sound like me?

It starts from your words, so it has your ideas and phrases to work with. Talk it through or paste your notes, then edit the draft until it reads the way you talk. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

Is the voice recording kept?

No. Briopost turns the recording into text and doesn’t keep it. You can talk for up to three minutes, or type instead.

What is a good hook for a LinkedIn post?

A first line that makes a specific promise or names a problem people recognize, written the way you would say it out loud.

What should I post on LinkedIn?

Something you learned, a mistake you fixed, a question clients keep asking, or a number from your own work. Briopost’s weekly ideas give you a starting list for your field.

Does Briopost post for me?

Never on its own. Every post waits for your OK, and nothing goes to LinkedIn until you confirm it yourself. See what we never do.
