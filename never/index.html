What we never do

Last updated November 15, 2026

Your LinkedIn account is worth more than any post. These are the promises Briopost keeps to protect it, in plain words.

We never post without your OK

Nothing goes to LinkedIn until you press confirm on that exact post. If you change the post afterward, the confirmation is cleared and you confirm again. The AI assistant and any connected assistant app can draft and schedule, but they cannot confirm for you. Only you, signed in, can.

We never scrape LinkedIn or act as you

Briopost is a website, not a browser extension. You connect LinkedIn on LinkedIn's own sign-in page, and Briopost uses LinkedIn's official sign-in and posting tools. We never see your LinkedIn password, and we do not scrape LinkedIn or log in as you.

We never like, comment on or message other people

Briopost does not send connection requests or direct messages, and it does not like or comment on anyone else's posts. The only comments or reshares it sends are ones you wrote yourself for your own post, and they are part of what you confirm.

We never sell your information

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. See the Privacy Policy.

We never keep your LinkedIn connection in plain text

The keys that let Briopost post for you are stored encrypted (AES-256), used only to publish what you confirm, and removed when you disconnect LinkedIn, or with the rest of your account when it is deleted.

We never show your private notes in a share link

A share link shows your slides and nothing else. The text you pasted in, your slide notes, your suggested caption and your private brand-kit notes are never included.

We never hide what AI costs

Before each AI action, Briopost shows you what it will cost in credits.

We never make it hard to leave

Delete your account yourself from the Account page, any time. It is held for 30 days in case you change your mind, and then it is deleted for good.

Questions? Write to info@briopost.com. Briopost is made by Briopost LLC and is independent of LinkedIn. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
