Everything Briopost does

One studio for LinkedIn posts: write it, design it, plan it, and give your OK. Here’s the whole list, grouped the way you’ll use it.

Create

Start from whatever you have. Every AI step shows its cost first.

  • From a few words

    Type a few words and get a designed carousel you can edit.

  • From a link

    Paste a link and Briopost turns the article into slides.

  • From a PDF

    Upload a PDF and start from what it says.

  • From your notes

    Paste your rough notes and get a first draft.

  • From your photos

    Drop in your photos and they fill the picture slots.

  • Talk it through

    Talk for up to three minutes and get a post in your own voice. The recording isn’t kept.

  • Text posts and polls

    Write text posts and polls, not only carousels.

  • Graphics

    Make a single graphic for a post, a story or your profile banner.

  • Ideas every Monday

    Pick your field and get fresh post ideas every Monday. Follow the blogs you read and turn new articles into ideas.

  • A ready week of drafts

    Get a ready week of drafts each Monday. Nothing goes out until you OK it.

  • The assistant

    Ask the built-in assistant to plan, write or fix a carousel.

  • 13 small tools

    Post preview, character counter, hook and headline ideas and more.

Design

Real design range, in your own brand kit. Your words stay readable on every color.

  • 150 carousel designs

    Each one takes your colors, fonts and logo.

  • 100 graphic designs

    For a post, a story or your profile banner.

  • 21 industry hubs

    With 69 example decks to start from.

  • 32 photo styles

    For your own photos.

  • 6 chart kinds

    From your own numbers.

  • About 200 icons

    In line, duotone or solid.

  • 40 fonts

    Served by Briopost.

  • 10 ready color sets

    Or your own, in a brand kit.

  • 21 ready slide layouts

    For lists, quotes, numbers and more.

  • Brand kit

    Save your colors, fonts and logo once; every design follows them. Start it from your website address.

  • Type on the slide

    Click any word on the slide and type. Highlight the words that matter.

  • Charts

    Add bar, line, area, pie, radar and gauge charts from your own numbers.

  • Comparisons and cards

    Show before and after, or do and don’t, with ticks and crosses. Group points into cards.

  • Big numbers, quotes, lists

    Make one number the whole slide. Quotes, lists, tip boxes and buttons are one click away.

  • Pictures

    Search stock photos without leaving the editor, or make a picture with AI. You see the cost first.

  • Picture controls

    Drag to frame your photo, then tint or shade it. Keep your photos in one library.

  • Sizes

    Carousels in square, portrait or story size; graphics also in wide and banner sizes.

  • Your own templates

    Save any carousel as your own template. Every slide can carry your name, website and page number.

Plan and approve

Every post sits on a calendar and waits for your OK.

  • Calendar

    See every post on a calendar, by month, week, day or as a list.

  • Post with your OK

    Connect your LinkedIn profile and schedule posts. Nothing posts without your OK.

  • One list to approve

    Everything waiting for your OK sits in one list.

  • A series in one go

    Plan a run of posts in one go. They land as drafts.

  • Rhythm and nudges

    Set your posting rhythm and get a nudge when a slot is empty. Set a weekly goal and keep your streak.

  • Repeats

    Repeat a good post with fresh wording. Each repeat waits for your OK.

  • Comments

    Add a first comment that goes out with the post, and plan follow-up comments for after.

  • Client review links

    Send a review link. Clients approve or comment without an account.

  • Share and export

    Share a carousel with a link, or embed it on your site. Download as a PDF, as images or as a short video.

Measure

Your numbers, from LinkedIn’s own file.

  • Your analytics file

    Upload LinkedIn’s own analytics file and see your numbers in Briopost.

  • Monthly report card

    Get a simple monthly report card.

  • What AI cost

    See what each AI action cost.

Stay in control

You decide what posts, what it costs, and what stays.

  • Nothing posts without your OK

    Every post waits for your confirm click.

  • Cost first

    You see what an AI action will cost before you run it.

  • Standard or Best

    Choose Standard or Best for each AI action.

  • A monthly allowance

    The beta is free, with 300 AI credits a month.

  • Your own key

    Use your own AI key if you prefer.

  • Leave any time

    Download everything you made, or delete your account, any time.

  • Tell us

    Tell us what to fix with the Feedback button.

Connect

Work from the tools you already use.

  • Your AI assistant

    Connect your AI assistant and work on your carousels from there.

  • Send to Briopost

    Send any web page to Briopost with one bookmark. No extension to install.

  • An API

    There’s an API for your own tools, with webhooks.

Teams

Bring the people who help you post.

  • Roles

    Invite your team. Owners, admins and members each get the right controls.

  • Workspaces

    Work in more than one workspace, for example one per client.
