Briopost vs Canva for LinkedIn carousels: an honest comparison

Canva is a design tool for almost anything, and LinkedIn carousels are one of the things it makes. Briopost is a studio made only for LinkedIn posts, now in a free beta. Both can get a good carousel onto LinkedIn. They suit different people.

Our verdict

Our verdict: for LinkedIn posts, choose Briopost

Canva is the better tool for designing everything. For LinkedIn posts, we think Briopost is the better choice. We make Briopost, so here are the reasons, each one checkable in the table below.

  • It writes the post, not only the slides. Start from a few words, notes, a link, a PDF or a voice note, and get a carousel drafted in your brand that you edit.
  • Everything LinkedIn needs in one place. Carousels, graphics, text posts and polls, a first comment and follow-up comments, all on one calendar. Canva doesn’t list text posts or polls.
  • Posting is free during the beta. Canva schedules to LinkedIn from its Pro plan, $18 a month. The Briopost beta is free, with posting and 300 AI credits a month.
  • You stay in control. Each AI action shows its cost first, and nothing posts without your confirm click.

Pick Canva if you design for many channels, want the biggest template library, or post to a Company Page today.

The short answer

If you design for many places (Instagram, presentations, print, video) and LinkedIn is one of them, Canva is hard to beat. It has a very large template library, a free plan, and it can schedule to LinkedIn and other networks on its paid plans.

If LinkedIn is the only place you post and you want help with the words as well as the slides, Briopost is built for that. It starts from a few words, notes, a link, a PDF or a voice note, drafts the carousel in your brand, shows the AI cost before each step, and never posts anything without your OK.

We make Briopost, so read this with that in mind. We’ve tried to say plainly where Canva is the better choice.

What Canva does well

  • Range. Canva’s LinkedIn carousel template page lists 1,294+ templates, and its paid plans list 3.6M+ templates in all.
  • A free plan. Canva Free costs US$0 and includes one brand kit (up to three colors) and an editor with 1,000+ design types.
  • Every channel in one place. Canva’s Content Planner can schedule a design to LinkedIn, Facebook Pages, Instagram business accounts, X, Pinterest, Slack and Tumblr.
  • Teams. Canva Business lists collaboration and team admin tools, approvals and up to 100 brand kits.

What Briopost does well

  • Made for LinkedIn. Carousels, single graphics, text posts and polls, from 150 carousel designs and 100 graphic designs, all in your brand kit.
  • Words and design together. Start from a few words, notes, a link, a PDF or up to three minutes of talking, and get a first draft you edit.
  • Cost first. You see what an AI action will cost before you run it, and you choose Standard or Best each time.
  • Your OK, every time. Every post waits for your confirm click. Clients can approve or comment on a review link without an account.

Which one should you pick?

Pick Canva if…

  • You design for many channels, not only LinkedIn.
  • You want the largest choice of templates, photos and elements.
  • You need a free plan that stays free.
  • You post to a LinkedIn Company Page today.
  • Your team already works in Canva.

Choose Briopost if…

  • LinkedIn is where you post, and you post as yourself.
  • You want the words drafted from your notes, a link, a PDF or a voice note, not only the design.
  • You want to see what each AI action costs before it runs.
  • You want text posts, polls and carousels on one calendar.
  • Clients need to approve posts without making an account.

Questions

Canva and Briopost questions

Is Canva good for LinkedIn carousels?

Yes. Canva lists 1,294+ LinkedIn carousel templates and can download a design as a PDF, which is the usual file for a LinkedIn carousel. On its paid plans it can also schedule the post to LinkedIn.

Is Briopost a Canva alternative?

For LinkedIn posts, yes. Briopost designs carousels and graphics in your brand and also drafts the words. For anything outside LinkedIn, such as print, video or other networks, Canva does far more.

How much do Canva and Briopost cost?

On November 25, 2026, Canva listed Free at US$0, Pro at US$18 a month for one person and Business at US$25 a month per person, before tax. Briopost is free during its beta, by invitation, and will share pricing before anyone pays.

Can I post to a LinkedIn Company Page?

Canva Help mentions connecting LinkedIn Pages to its Content Planner. Briopost can’t post to Company Pages yet; that waits on LinkedIn’s approval. Today Briopost posts to your own LinkedIn profile, with your OK.

Does Briopost post without asking me?

No. Every post waits on your calendar until you confirm it. If you change a post after confirming it, you confirm again.
