Good to know

How to plan a carousel that reads well.

One idea, said on slide 1

The first slide is the hook: it tells people what they get for swiping. Make it a promise the slides keep, such as “Five questions to ask before your first hire”.

One point per slide

Each slide should make one point someone can read in a few seconds. If a point needs a second sentence to explain the first, it may be two slides.

Keep the words few

Most people swipe carousels on a phone. As a rough guide, keep each slide under about 25 to 30 words and let the design do the rest. A short phrase in big type beats a paragraph in small type.

End with a next step

The last slide tells readers what to do now: answer a question in the comments, save the post, or read the next one. One clear ask is enough.

Pick the shape before you design

Portrait 4:5 (1080 x 1350) fills more of a phone screen and suits most carousels. Square 1:1 (1080 x 1080) and tall 9:16 (1080 x 1920) also work. Keep every slide the same size. The carousel size guide explains the choice.

How long can a carousel be?

Long enough to make your points and no longer. LinkedIn’s own limit is far beyond that: its Help page on uploading and sharing documents says “The file size cannot exceed 100MB and 300 pages.” (checked November 17, 2026).

Questions

Planning questions

How many slides should a LinkedIn carousel have?

There is no fixed number. Plan a hook, one slide per point and a next step, then cut any slide that repeats another. The planner counts them for you.

How many words should go on a carousel slide?

As a guideline, keep most slides under about 25 to 30 words so they read easily on a phone. It is not a LinkedIn rule. When a slide runs longer, split it into two.

Which slide shape should I choose?

For most carousels, 4:5 portrait at 1080 x 1350. Square 1080 x 1080 and tall 1080 x 1920 also work. The planner draws the outlines in the shape you pick.

Is my plan sent or saved anywhere?

No. The planner runs in your browser. Nothing you type leaves your device or is stored, so copy your plan before you close the page.
