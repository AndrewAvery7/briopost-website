Carousel tool scorecard

Most carousel tools make a good-looking slide. Score up to three on the ten questions that matter later, such as whether anything posts without your OK, and see which comes out ahead.

Runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored.

    How to use it

    Compare carousel tools in four steps.

    1. 1

      Name the tools

      Type the names of up to three tools you’re trying. “Add a tool” adds another; “Add Briopost’s answers” fills one in as our checklist states them.

    2. 2

      Answer each question

      For each of the ten questions, choose Yes, Partly or No for every tool. “What to look for” opens that question in the full checklist.

    3. 3

      Read the scores

      Yes counts 1 and Partly counts half, out of 10. A tool that can post without your OK is flagged as a deal-breaker, whatever its score.

    4. 4

      Copy and share

      Choose “Copy the scorecard” to paste every answer and score into an email or a note for your team.

    Before and after

    Same two tools; the questions show the difference the slides hide. The full checklist explains each question and what to look for.

    Good to know

    What the scorecard weighs.

    Why these ten questions

    They cover what goes wrong after the first good-looking slide: posting without your OK, surprise AI costs, your brand, editing, exports, approvals, privacy, your data, teams and what LinkedIn allows.

    A score is a summary

    Yes counts 1 and Partly counts half, so a tool can score up to 10. One No on a question that matters to you can outweigh several Yes answers, so read the answers as well as the number.

    The one deal-breaker

    A tool that can post without your OK is flagged whatever its score, and left out of the lead. A post goes out under your name.

    Briopost’s own answers

    “Add Briopost’s answers” uses what our checklist says Briopost does today, including the one Partly: posting to Company Pages waits on LinkedIn’s approval. Check them yourself in the free beta.

    Questions

    Scorecard questions

    How is the score worked out?

    Each Yes counts 1 and each Partly counts half, across ten questions, so the most a tool can score is 10. A question you haven’t answered counts 0 until you do.

    Why is a tool left out of the lead?

    A tool that can post without your OK is flagged as a deal-breaker and left out of the lead, whatever its score, because a post goes out under your name.

    Can I compare more than three tools?

    The scorecard compares up to three at a time. Copy the scorecard, clear it and score the next ones.

    Is anything I type sent or saved?

    No. The scorecard runs in your browser. Tool names and answers never leave your device and are not stored, so copy the scorecard before you close the page.
