Free LinkedIn tools

Four small tools for the everyday parts of posting on LinkedIn. They are free, need no account, and run in your browser: nothing you type or pick is sent anywhere.

  • LinkedIn post formatter

    Make words bold or italic, add bullet and numbered lists, see where the feed cuts your post with “…see more”, and count characters against the limit.

    Runs in your browser; nothing is sent.

    Open the post formatter

  • LinkedIn carousel size checker

    Pick a picture or a PDF and see its size, its shape and its page count, and whether it matches the portrait, square or story carousel sizes.

    Runs in your browser; nothing is sent.

    Open the carousel size checker

  • LinkedIn hook preview

    Write up to three versions of your opening and see each one in a phone-width feed preview, with roughly where “…see more” cuts it, the character count and the lines that show.

    Runs in your browser; nothing is sent.

    Open the hook preview

  • Carousel slide planner

    Type one idea and your points, one per line, and get a slide-by-slide carousel plan with a word count on every slide and outlines in 4:5, 1:1 or 9:16.

    Runs in your browser; nothing is sent.

    Open the slide planner

Planning the slides themselves? Read what size to make a LinkedIn carousel or how to make one, step by step.
