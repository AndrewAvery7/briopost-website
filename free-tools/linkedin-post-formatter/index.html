How to use it

Format a LinkedIn post in four steps.

  1. 1

    Write or paste your post

    Type in the box above, or paste a draft you already have. Not sure where to start? Choose “Try an example” to fill in a sample post.

  2. 2

    Style a few words

    Select a word or phrase, then choose Bold or Italic (or press Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I). Select some lines and choose Bullets or Numbers to turn them into a list. “Remove styling” puts selected text back to plain.

  3. 3

    Check the preview

    Keep the counter under about 3,000 characters. Switch between Phone and Computer to see roughly where “…see more” cuts the post, and keep your main point above it.

  4. 4

    Copy and paste into LinkedIn

    Choose “Copy post”, start a new post on LinkedIn and paste. The bold and italic words, lists and line breaks come with it.

Good to know

How the formatting works.

Bold and italic are special letters

LinkedIn posts are plain text. Bold and italic come from look-alike letters in the Unicode “mathematical” set, so they show up everywhere your post goes. Screen readers may read them oddly or spell them out letter by letter, and search may not match them. Use them sparingly: a few words for emphasis, never a whole post.

The length limit

A LinkedIn post can hold about 3,000 characters. The counter turns red when you go past that. Styled letters take up more space behind the scenes, so the counter also tells you the longer count when your post has any.

The “…see more” cut

Most people read only what shows before “…see more”. The preview cuts after about three lines, or sooner when the opening runs long, which is close to what the feed does. Switch between Phone and Computer to see both.

Lists and line breaks

Bullets use the • character and numbered lists use “1.”, both plain text that LinkedIn keeps. Empty lines stay in place, so short paragraphs come through as you typed them.
