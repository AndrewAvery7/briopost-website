LinkedIn carousel size: what to use and why

Use 1080 by 1350 pixels, a 4:5 portrait shape, unless you have a reason not to. It fills more of a phone screen than a square, and every Briopost carousel design comes in it.

Why 4:5 suits phones

Most people scroll the LinkedIn feed on a phone held upright. A 4:5 slide is 1,350 pixels tall for every 1,080 across, so at the same width it is a quarter taller than a square. It takes up more of the screen while people swipe, and that extra height lets you set the same words in bigger type.

A square slide still works, and it can be easier to reuse elsewhere. You give up some height. If you try the tall 9:16 size, check how one test document looks in the feed before you make a whole series in it.

Whatever you choose, keep every slide in a carousel the same size.

How to export a sharp PDF

  • Export at the full size, 1080 pixels wide, straight from your design tool.
  • Don’t build the PDF from screenshots. That is the most common cause of blurry slides.
  • Keep every page the same size and shape.
  • Check that your fonts came through, then open the PDF on your phone once.

Briopost builds the PDF slide by slide at the size you picked, so the export matches what you saw in the editor. It can also export the slides as images or a short video.

What about LinkedIn’s own file limits?

LinkedIn sets its own limits on document uploads, such as file size, and it can change them. We don’t repeat those numbers here. Before you post a very large file, check LinkedIn Help’s page on uploading and sharing documents.

Some figures you’ll find around the web were written for LinkedIn’s carousel ads, which are a separate product with their own rules. They don’t apply to a document post.

How many slides to use is covered in the step-by-step guide. LinkedIn’s current document limits, quoted from LinkedIn Help, are in how to post a PDF carousel.

Questions

Size questions

What size should a LinkedIn carousel be?

1080 by 1350 pixels, a 4:5 portrait shape, for most posts. 1080 by 1080 (square) also works. Briopost makes both, plus a tall 1080 by 1920 size.

Is 4:5 or 1:1 better for a LinkedIn carousel?

For most posts, 4:5. At the same width it is a quarter taller than a square, so it fills more of a phone screen and leaves room for bigger type.

What file type does a LinkedIn carousel use?

A PDF is the usual choice. LinkedIn turns the pages of the document into the slides people swipe through.

Why does my carousel look blurry on LinkedIn?

The slides were probably exported smaller than full size, or the PDF was built from screenshots. Export at 1080 pixels wide and keep every page the same size.
