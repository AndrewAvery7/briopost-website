An aiCarousels alternative: how Briopost compares

aiCarousels is a popular AI carousel maker for LinkedIn and other apps. If you are weighing it against Briopost, here is what each one does, what each costs, and who each one suits, in plain terms.

By the Briopost team · Last updated November 25, 2026 · Facts about aiCarousels come from its own website, checked November 25, 2026.

What aiCarousels is

aiCarousels is an AI carousel maker. You give it an idea, a link, a PDF, a YouTube video or your notes, and its AI writes and designs a carousel you can edit. Besides carousels, it makes image posts, video posts and text posts.

It can post to your own LinkedIn profile, now or at a time you pick. For Instagram, TikTok, X and Threads, it gives you downloads sized for each app. It has a Pro plan for one person, a Team plan for five people, and a one-time Pro Lifetime option that runs on your own OpenAI key.

What aiCarousels does well

  • A wide range of designs. Its pricing page lists 304 layouts and 95 themes, with charts, mockups and timelines.
  • Many writing voices. 19 “ghostwriters” write slides and captions in different styles, from founder stories to satire.
  • More than one app. The same carousel can be resized for LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X and Threads.
  • A pay-once option. Pro Lifetime costs $99 once, with the AI billed by OpenAI on your own key.
  • Free tools without an account, such as a LinkedIn text formatter and a PDF carousel checker.

How Briopost is different

Briopost is a studio made only for LinkedIn. It is in a free beta, by invitation. Here is where it takes a different path.

  • Every post waits for your OK. aiCarousels can post at the time you pick on its own. In Briopost, a scheduled post goes out only after you confirm it, and if you change it after confirming, you confirm again.
  • You see the cost before each AI action. aiCarousels counts AI carousels, pictures and edits against a monthly allowance. Briopost shows what each action will cost before it runs, and lets you choose Standard or Best each time.
  • Client review links. Clients can approve or comment on posts through a link, without making an account.
  • More than carousels, for LinkedIn only. Briopost makes carousels, graphics, text posts and polls, and plans them on a calendar with series, repeats, a first comment and follow-up comments. It does not post to other apps.
  • It is still in beta. Briopost is free while the beta runs, by invitation. aiCarousels is an established paid product.

Which one fits you

Stay with aiCarousels if…

  • You post the same carousels to Instagram, TikTok, X or Threads as well as LinkedIn.
  • You want a paid plan with a fixed monthly allowance today, or a one-time payment on your own OpenAI key.
  • You like choosing from many writing voices and a large set of layouts.
  • You are happy for scheduled posts to go out on their own at the time you set.

Try Briopost if…

  • You want every post to wait for your OK before it goes out.
  • You want to see what each AI action costs before you run it.
  • You post for clients and want them to approve through a link, without an account.
  • You want carousels, graphics, text posts and polls planned on one LinkedIn calendar.
  • You are happy to use a product in beta, for free, and tell us what is missing.

Questions

aiCarousels alternative questions

Is Briopost a good aiCarousels alternative?

It depends on what you need. Briopost is made only for LinkedIn and asks for your OK before every post. aiCarousels also prepares carousels for Instagram, TikTok, X and Threads and can post on its own at a time you set. The table on this page shows the differences.

How much does aiCarousels cost?

Its pricing page lists Pro at $120 a year (about $10 a month), Team at $468 a year for 5 people, and Pro Lifetime at $99 once with your own OpenAI key. Month-to-month prices are also offered; check its site for the current figures.

How much does Briopost cost?

Briopost is free during the beta, by invitation, with 300 AI credits a month. Pricing will be shared before anyone pays.

Can either tool post to a LinkedIn Company Page?

Not today. aiCarousels says company pages aren’t offered and suggests downloading the PDF. In Briopost, posting to Company Pages waits on LinkedIn’s approval.

Can I bring my own AI key?

In aiCarousels, Pro Lifetime runs on your own OpenAI key. In Briopost you can use your own Claude or OpenAI key, or a service that works like OpenAI’s.
