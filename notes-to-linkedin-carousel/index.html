Turn rough notes into a LinkedIn carousel

Messy notes are a fine place to start. Find the one idea in them, give each point its own slide, and turn your shorthand into words a stranger can follow. Here is how, and how Briopost helps.

From notes to carousel in seven steps

  1. Find the one idea
  2. Group the notes, then cut
  3. One point per slide, usually 6 to 10 slides
  4. Write for someone who wasn’t there
  5. Credit the people behind the ideas
  6. Write a new hook
  7. End with one next step

  1. 01

    Find the one idea

    Read your notes from top to bottom and ask what you’d tell a colleague in one sentence. That sentence is your carousel.

    Notes from a meeting, a talk or a long walk usually hold more than one idea. Pick one, and keep the rest for later posts.

  2. 02

    Group the notes, then cut

    Put related lines together. Each group that supports your sentence can become a slide. Lines that don’t support it go on a list for another day.

  3. 03

    One point per slide, usually 6 to 10 slides

    Give each group one slide: a short heading and a sentence or two. With a cover and a last slide, most carousels land between 6 and 10 slides.

    The idea sets the count, not a rule. Three lessons make a short carousel; a step-by-step method needs more room. If you have more than ten groups, you may have two carousels.

  4. 04

    Write for someone who wasn’t there

    Notes are shorthand only you can read. Turn each point into a full sentence, spell out abbreviations, and add the example that made it click for you.

    Check any number or quote you wrote from memory before it goes on a slide.

  5. 05

    Credit the people behind the ideas

    If the notes come from a talk, a book or a colleague, name them. If you’re summarizing someone else’s talk or course, check they’re happy for you to share it.

    Meeting notes can hold private details about clients or coworkers. Take those out, and don’t share anything said in confidence.

  6. 06

    Write a new hook

    Your notes probably start with a date or a meeting name. The cover needs something else: what the reader gets, in a few words. For example, “Notes from the planning offsite” could become “Three questions we now ask before every project.”

  7. 07

    End with one next step

    Ask for one thing on the last slide: a reply with the reader’s own experience, a save, or a follow for your next post.

How to plan the slides, step by step

  1. 1

    Write your idea

    Type it in “Your idea (slide 1)”. It becomes the cover, so say what people get for swiping.

  2. 2

    Add your points

    Type one point per line. Each line becomes a slide, and bullets or numbers at the start of a line are left off.

  3. 3

    Check the plan

    Pick 4:5, 1:1 or 9:16 to see the outlines. A slide with an orange border may be hard to read on a phone: split it into two lines.

  4. 4

    Add the next step and copy

    Say what readers should do after the last slide, then choose “Copy as plain text” and take the plan into your design tool or Briopost.

Questions

Notes to carousel questions

Can I make a LinkedIn carousel from rough notes?

Yes. Find the one idea in them, group the rest into points, and give each point its own slide. Briopost can turn pasted notes into a first draft you edit.

How many slides should it have?

Often 6 to 10, but the idea sets the count. A cover, one slide per point and a last slide is enough.

Do my notes need to be tidy?

No. Bullet points and half sentences are fine. Take out anything private before you paste them.

Does it cost anything?

Drafting with AI costs money, so Briopost shows about what it will cost before it runs. During the beta, that comes out of your included AI credits.

Will Briopost post it for me?

Not on its own. Every post waits for your OK, and nothing goes to LinkedIn until you confirm it yourself.

How do I turn my notes into slides?

Type the one idea, then one point per line, into the free slide planner on this page. Each line becomes a slide, and any slide with too many words for a phone is flagged.
