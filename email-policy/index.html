Email Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Download as PDF

Briopost is preparing to open to customers. This document describes how Briopost works for customers from the day accounts open, and how this website works today.

1. What we send

Briopost sends service email only, from planner@briopost.com, hello@briopost.com and info@briopost.com:

  • account messages (sign-in, security and account changes, and a notice if your LinkedIn connection needs reconnecting);
  • reminders you switch on, such as a nudge to plan this week's LinkedIn post, or a reminder to confirm a post that is waiting for you;
  • a weekly summary of post ideas, if you turn it on;
  • a Friday progress email once you set a weekly posting goal: how many posts you published against your goal, your streak, what is scheduled and one tip. It is on when you set a goal, and you can turn it off in Settings (Posting goal) or with the link in the email, without changing your goal;
  • an email when your weekly plan is ready, if you switched on the weekly plan and its "Email me when my week is ready" option. It says how many drafts are waiting, never what they say;
  • billing messages about your own subscription: a notice before your free trial ends, receipts and refunds, a notice if a payment fails, and a reminder a few days before a break you chose ends. These are account messages and have no unsubscribe link, but you can stop them by canceling your subscription;
  • after a paid plan ends, up to two short reminders (about 30 and 60 days later) that your work is still in your account and how to restart, export or delete it. They mention the current plans and prices but carry no discounts, are sent only to former customers, and have a one-click unsubscribe link;
  • help messages when you start, such as a welcome and one setup tip about using your own AI key instead of credits, which you can turn off;
  • waitlist emails, if you join the waitlist on briopost.com: the message asking you to confirm your email address, which we send because you asked to join; and one message when Briopost opens, plus invitations to early access if we offer them;
  • replies to messages you send us.

2. What we do not send

No marketing or newsletters without a separate opt-in. The only exception is the two reminders after a paid plan ends (section 1), which go only to former customers and stop with one click. Joining the waitlist signs you up for the waitlist emails above and nothing else. We never buy, rent, scrape or share email lists. Briopost does not send email to your audience for you: it helps you publish posts to LinkedIn, and has no feature for bulk email.

3. Consent

You only receive reminders and summaries because you switched them on in Briopost, or set a weekly posting goal, which includes the Friday progress email. You can switch each of them off in Briopost or with the link in the email. Account and billing messages are sent because you have an account. Waitlist emails use a double opt-in: we email you a confirmation link when you join, and you only go on the list when you click it. If you never confirm, we send nothing more unless you sign up again, and we delete the entry after 30 days.

4. Stopping email

Every reminder and summary says how to change or turn it off. You can also reply to any message, or write to info@briopost.com, and we will stop it. Once you opt out, we stop sending that kind of email to you.

Every waitlist email, every optional email (setup tips, the Friday progress email and the "week is ready" email), and every reminder after a paid plan ends, has a one-click unsubscribe link, and mail programs that show an unsubscribe button can use it too. Unsubscribing takes effect right away, and we never take longer than 10 business days.

5. Bounces and complaints

If an address cannot receive mail, or someone reports a message as unwanted, we stop sending that kind of email to that address. We monitor delivery and act on complaints promptly.

6. Sender identity and delivery

Our email is sent from briopost.com and authenticated with SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Delivery is handled by Mailgun. Mail you send to planner@briopost.com or info@briopost.com is received through Cloudflare Email Routing and read by our team.

7. Reporting a problem

If you received something you did not expect, or believe someone is misusing Briopost to send unwanted email, write to info@briopost.com with the message attached. We investigate every report.
