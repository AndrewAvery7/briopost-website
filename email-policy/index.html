Email Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Download as PDF

Briopost is preparing to open to customers. This document describes how Briopost works for customers from the day accounts open, and how this website works today.

1. What we send

Briopost sends service email only, from planner@briopost.com and info@briopost.com:

  • account messages (sign-in, security and account changes);
  • reminders you switch on, such as a nudge to plan this week's LinkedIn post, or a reminder to confirm a post that is waiting for you;
  • a weekly summary of post ideas, if you turn it on;
  • replies to messages you send us.

2. What we do not send

No marketing or newsletters without a separate opt-in. We never buy, rent, scrape or share email lists. Briopost does not send email to your audience for you: it helps you publish posts to LinkedIn, and has no feature for bulk email.

3. Consent

You only receive reminders and summaries because you switched them on in Briopost, and you can switch them off in the same place. Account messages are sent because you have an account.

4. Stopping email

Every reminder and summary says how to change or turn it off. You can also reply to any message, or write to info@briopost.com, and we will stop it. Once you opt out, we stop sending that kind of email to you.

5. Bounces and complaints

If an address cannot receive mail, or someone reports a message as unwanted, we stop sending that kind of email to that address. We monitor delivery and act on complaints promptly.

6. Sender identity and delivery

Our email is sent from briopost.com and authenticated with SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Delivery is handled by Mailgun. Mail you send to planner@briopost.com or info@briopost.com is received through Cloudflare Email Routing and read by our team.

7. Reporting a problem

If you received something you did not expect, or believe someone is misusing Briopost to send unwanted email, write to info@briopost.com with the message attached. We investigate every report.
