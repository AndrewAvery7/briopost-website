When one graphic beats a carousel

A carousel is for an idea that needs steps. A single graphic is for an idea that fits on one screen. Reach for a graphic when you have:

  • One line worth remembering. A quote, a rule you work by, a short opinion.
  • One number. A result from your own work or a figure from a source you can name.
  • A short list. Three to five tips that each fit on one line.
  • An announcement. An event, a launch date, a new role.

The post text does the explaining. The graphic gives the post one clear thing to look at. To draft that text from a rough thought, try the post generator.

Need more than one screen?

If you find yourself shrinking the type to fit it all in, the idea wants to be a carousel. Briopost can build a carousel around a graphic you’ve made.

LinkedIn graphic sizes

Briopost graphics come in five sizes. For a post in the feed, portrait or square are the usual choices.

  • Portrait1080 × 1350
  • Square1080 × 1080
  • Story1080 × 1920
  • Landscape1920 × 1080
  • Profile banner1584 × 396

Portrait, a 4:5 shape, is a quarter taller than a square at the same width, so it takes up more of a phone screen and leaves room for bigger type. The profile banner is for the top of your LinkedIn profile: you download it and add it there yourself. The LinkedIn carousel size guide explains the shapes in more detail.

What makes a graphic easy to read on a phone

Most people will see your graphic on a small screen, at a glance, while scrolling. Design for that moment.

  • Few words. One line someone can read at a glance, plus a short second line if you need it. The rest goes in the post text.
  • Big type. If you have to zoom in to read it, so will everyone else.
  • Strong contrast. Dark text on a light background or light on dark. Text laid over a busy photo needs a calm area or a shaded band behind it.
  • One idea. One quote, one number or one list. A second idea is a second post.
  • Room at the edges. Keep words away from the borders so nothing feels cramped or gets cut off.
  • Your name, small. A handle or website in a corner tells people where the graphic came from when it’s shared.

Briopost checks contrast for you. Its readability guard keeps text at a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 with its background (3 to 1 for big type), so your brand colors stay readable.

Six kinds of LinkedIn graphics

Each one carries a different kind of idea. Pick the kind first, then the design.

  • Quote

    One line you said, or one you heard and can credit. Put the name under it.

  • One big number

    The number large, and a short line saying what it measures and where it came from.

  • Tips

    Three to five short tips, one line each. Number them if the order matters.

  • Before and after

    Two sides: the old way and the new way. Keep both sides the same length.

  • Question

    A question in big type, with the answers invited in the comments.

  • Event

    What, when and where, in that order. Put the sign-up link in the post text.

Questions

Graphic questions

What size should a LinkedIn graphic be?

For a post in the feed, 1080 by 1350 pixels (portrait) or 1080 by 1080 (square). Portrait takes up more of a phone screen. Briopost also makes story, landscape and profile banner sizes.

Should I post a graphic or a carousel?

Use a graphic for one idea that fits on one screen, such as a quote, a number or a short list. Use a carousel when the idea needs steps.

Can my graphics use my brand colors and fonts?

Yes. Set up your brand kit once, and every graphic follows it. Briopost can read your colors and fonts from your website, or you can set them by hand.

Do the picture-led designs cost anything?

They paint a picture with AI, so they use AI credits. Briopost shows about what it will cost before it runs. During the beta, AI credits are included.

Can Briopost make a LinkedIn profile banner?

Yes, at 1584 by 396 pixels. You download the banner and add it to your profile yourself. Banners can’t be put on the calendar.

How do I check the size of a LinkedIn graphic?

Choose the image in the free size checker on this page, with Graphic selected. It shows the size and shape and says whether it matches one of the five graphic sizes: portrait, square, story, landscape or profile banner. Nothing is uploaded.
