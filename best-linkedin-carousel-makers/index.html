Our pick

Why we pick Briopost for most LinkedIn posters

Most tools on this list make carousels and add scheduling or growth features around them. Briopost is built for one job: getting posts that sound like you and look like your brand onto LinkedIn. Here is what that gives you, measured against the table above.

  • The most generous free use on this list. The beta is free, with 300 AI credits a month: enough for dozens of AI-written carousels (pictures use more). Exports carry no watermark, and posting to LinkedIn is included. Compare Blendin Free at 5 pieces a month, PostNitro Free at 5 downloads with a watermark, and aiCarousels’ one free try with no download.
  • More than carousels, from one brand kit. Carousels from 150 designs, single graphics from 100 designs in five sizes (including the profile banner), text posts and polls, all on one calendar.
  • It starts from what you already have. A few words, notes, a link, a PDF or up to three minutes of talking becomes a first draft you edit.
  • You see the cost before you click. Every AI action shows what it will cost before it runs, and you choose Standard or Best. We found no other tool here that shows that.
  • Nothing goes out without your OK. Every post waits for your confirm click, and Briopost never likes, comments on or messages anyone for you. In aiCarousels, for example, a scheduled post goes out on its own at the time you set.
  • Client approval without the price tag. Clients approve or comment through a review link, with no account. On Supergrow, approval workflows start with the $199-a-month Teams plan.

Where Briopost is not the right pick: if you post to Instagram or TikTok too, post to a Company Page today, want automated engagement or lead outreach, or need a product that is out of beta. The situations below name the tool that fits each of those.

By situation

Which one should you pick?

Start from what matters most to you. Briopost is our pick for most people; these are also the situations where we’d point you elsewhere.

You mainly post on LinkedIn, as yourself

Briopost, our pick. Carousels, graphics, text posts and polls in your brand, drafted from your notes or voice, free during the beta, and nothing posts without your OK.

You already design in Canva

Canva. Your templates, brand kit and files are already there, and Pro adds scheduling.

You want a free plan that can schedule

Blendin’s free plan includes 10 scheduled posts a month. PostNitro’s free plan designs and exports but doesn’t schedule.

You want a quick free PDF

2pr.io turns a post, article or video link into a PDF carousel for free, with a watermark. PostNitro Free also exports, up to 5 slides.

You want AI to write it, at a low monthly price

aiCarousels Pro ($120 a year) or PostNitro Starter ($15 a month). aiCarousels also sells a one-time plan that runs on your own OpenAI key.

You want a full LinkedIn growth tool

Taplio or Supergrow: carousels sit beside writing, scheduling and analytics, from $39 a month.

You post on several networks

Blendin, Contentdrips and PostNitro all post to LinkedIn and to networks such as Instagram and TikTok.

Someone else approves your posts

Supergrow’s Teams plan has feedback and approval workflows. Briopost has client review links that need no account.

Nothing may post without your OK

Briopost: every post waits for your confirm, and each AI action shows its cost first. It is a free beta, by invitation.

Questions

Carousel maker questions

What is the best LinkedIn carousel maker?

For most people who post on LinkedIn as themselves, we pick Briopost: it makes carousels, graphics, text posts and polls in your brand, drafts them from your notes or voice, shows each AI cost first, and posts nothing without your OK. It is free during the beta. We make it, so try two or three tools against the same idea. If you also post to Instagram or TikTok, or want automated engagement, another tool on this page will fit better.

Is there a free LinkedIn carousel maker?

Yes. Canva, Blendin and PostNitro list free plans with limits. 2pr.io’s carousel generator is free, with a watermark on exports. aiCarousels offers one free try without a download, and Briopost is free during its beta, by invitation. Contentdrips, Supergrow and Taplio offer 7-day free trials instead.

Which carousel makers can schedule posts to LinkedIn?

aiCarousels, Blendin, Contentdrips, Supergrow and Taplio list scheduling to LinkedIn. PostNitro lists it from its Creator+ plan, and Canva from its Pro plan. Briopost schedules to your own profile, and each post waits for your OK. We could not find scheduling listed on 2pr.io’s pages.

Do I need a PDF to post a LinkedIn carousel?

A PDF is the usual way. LinkedIn shows the pages of a document as slides people swipe. Every tool on this list lists PDF export, and some can also post the carousel to LinkedIn for you.
